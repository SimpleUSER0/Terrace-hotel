# Photography and typography

TERRACE 17 is a fictional hotel concept. Photographs are illustrative stock assets, not the rooms, premises or staff of an actual business. The interiors come from different properties, including residential spaces. They are not an exact floor-plan or amenities representation. Photographers and depicted property owners are not presented as endorsing the project.

## Photography

All selected photographs are sourced from Unsplash and used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Copyright remains with the respective photographers. The images are included as part of a composed website concept, not a standalone stock-photo distribution product.

The original photographs were resized and converted to WebP. The mobile hero was cropped to fit a portrait screen. CSS crops some thumbnails; gallery lightboxes show the full image rendition.
